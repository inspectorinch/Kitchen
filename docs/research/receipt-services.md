Grocery suitability: no vendor page gives grocery-specific accuracy. Grocery receipts are the hard case (abbreviated names such as "ORG BNLS CHKN BRST", weights, discounts, multi-line items). Textract and Azure document line items generally; Veryfi markets receipts and line items. Treat all as unproven until the bake-off.

## Recommendation: two in the bake-off
1. **AWS Textract AnalyzeExpense**: cheapest official price ($0.01), clear line-item support, no minimums. Cost: needs an AWS account and the opt-out policy set so receipts are not used for service improvement. Check which API call, region, and whether synchronous calls take images (the FAQ did not say).
2. **Azure Document Intelligence prebuilt-receipt**: strongest privacy story of the five (24 hour deletion, delete call, 500 free pages a month for testing), documented line-item schema. Cost unknown until the owner reads the Azure calculator; if it is not about 1 cent a page, swap in Veryfi.
Not chosen: Google Document AI ($0.10 is 10x Textract, no free tier); Veryfi (retention controls are enterprise-only, 8x the price, but keep as the reserve since it is receipt-specialised); Mindee (pricing page inconsistent).
Also in the bake-off, per the architecture: "AI alone" (cheap vision model with schema) against "service then AI check". From the numbers in ai-vendors.md, a low-tier AI read costs under 0.1 cent, so a 1 cent dedicated service already costs about 10x the AI-alone read; the two-step pipeline has to earn that with accuracy. This may change the "receipt service first" decision in the spec; it is the owner's call after the bake-off.
